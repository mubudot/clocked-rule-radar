# Northwind Open AI Sprint — Official Rules (version of October 22, 2026)

## 1. Dates
The building period runs from October 22 to November 20, 2026. Submissions close on November 20, 2026 at 11:59 p.m. Pacific Time. Winners are announced on December 4, 2026.

## 2. Eligibility
The sprint is open to individuals aged 18 or older in any country where the contest is lawful. Employees of Northwind Labs and their immediate families may not enter.

## 3. Teams
You may enter alone or in a team of up to six people. Each person may be a member of only one team.

## 4. What to submit
Submit a project page with a description, a link to a source repository, and a demo video no longer than five minutes. The repository may be public or private; if private, share it with judges@northwind.example.

## 5. Prizes
The total prize pool is $10,000. First place receives $5,000, second place receives $3,000, and third place receives $2,000. Prizes are paid by bank transfer within 30 days of verification.

## 6. Judging
Projects are scored on technical quality (40%), originality (30%), and usefulness (30%).

## 7. Existing work
Projects started before the building period are allowed if the new work is clearly described.

## 8. Code of conduct
Harassment of any participant leads to disqualification.
