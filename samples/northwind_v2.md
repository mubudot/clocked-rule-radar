# Northwind Open AI Sprint — Official Rules (version of November 3, 2026)

## 1. Dates
The building period runs from October 22 to November 14, 2026. Submissions close on November 14, 2026 at 11:59 p.m. Pacific Time. Winners are announced on November 28, 2026.

## 2. Eligibility
The sprint is open to individuals aged 18 or older in any country where the contest is lawful, except residents of Quebec and of countries under US sanctions. Employees of Northwind Labs and their immediate families may not enter.

## 3. Teams
You may enter alone or in a team of up to four people. Each person may be a member of only one team.

## 4. What to submit
Submit a project page with a description, a link to a public source repository with its full commit history, and a demo video no longer than three minutes. Private repositories are not accepted.

## 5. Prizes
The total prize pool is $10,000. First place receives $6,000, second place receives $2,500, and third place receives $1,500. Prizes are paid through Wise within 30 days of verification; winners must submit a tax form before payment.

## 6. Judging
Projects are scored on technical quality (40%), originality (30%), and usefulness (30%).

## 7. Existing work
Only projects started during the building period are eligible. Earlier projects are not eligible, even with substantial changes.

## 8. Code of conduct
Harassment of any participant leads to disqualification.

## 9. Community award
A $500 community award goes to the project with the most votes on the Northwind forum. Voting is optional.
